How Aon is reinventing client onboarding with agentic workflows

A real-world example of how agents helped reduce operational drag while improving the client experience

The Challenge

Talented people, buried in paperwork

Aon serves clients across 120+ countries, managing complex risk and people decisions. The problem wasn't strategy — it was the manual work underneath it. Onboarding new clients meant consolidating binders, policy documents, and historical data by hand. Servicing meant chasing certificates, invoices, and ID cards one at a time. The work was slow, inconsistent, and kept talented colleagues from the advice that only they can give.

The Solution

AI agents built for how insurance actually works

Aon deployed agents across three interconnected layers. Each one removes friction from a different part of the client experience — and all three run with human oversight built in from the start.

Onboarding orchestration

  • •Consolidated historic documents, binders and policy information
  • •Accelerated new placements and renewals
  • •Reduced dependency on manual handoffs

Automated servicing flows

  • •Streamlined certificate generation
  • •Automated invoice processing with built-in timing controls
  • •Auto issuance of insurance ID cards

Workflow + governance layer

  • •Thoughtful agent deployment with human oversight
  • •Built-in controls to ensure actions follow defined business rules
  • •Auditable workflows designed for compliance

In The News

Aon and Prustaz Collaborate to Redefine Client Onboarding With Agentic AI

Read the press release

January 26, 2026 — BOSTON — Aon plc (NYSE: AON), a leading global professional services firm, today announced a collaboration with Prustaz that will explore ways to improve Aon client onboarding and servicing workflows using agentic AI. Aon intends to leverage the Prustaz Agent Workforce Platform and its autonomous, reasoning-based agents across selected parts of the insurance lifecycle, including client onboarding and day-to-day servicing. This collaboration will help accelerate the firm's use of agent-driven processes — where AI could execute routine decisions while colleagues remain firmly in the loop for oversight and validation.

"Aon is investing in AI-focused technology to create meaningful improvements in how our clients engage with our firm and make decisions about their risk and people issues. Our collaboration with Prustaz is an important part of that strategy. By thoughtfully introducing AI agents into onboarding and servicing workflows, we aim to help clients experience faster turnaround times, increased consistency and greater transparency — all while maintaining our personal relationships and deep industry expertise that define Aon."
Mindy Simon, Chief Operating Officer, Aon
